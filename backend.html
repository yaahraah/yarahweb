Back-End Development

Building the systems and processes that work behind a website.

What is Back-End Development?

Back-End Development focuses on the processes that happen behind a website or application. It involves handling data, processing requests, managing databases, and making sure that the system functions properly. Unlike Front-End Development, most back-end processes are not directly visible to users.
